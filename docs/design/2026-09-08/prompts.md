# Railbound design prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-08. Each concept received `current-board.png` as a functional reference. These are visual mockups; map routes and labels will use the existing game data during implementation.

## Shared brief

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one realistic, production-quality desktop web game UI mockup for Railbound, a Ticket to Ride USA game. Target 1600 x 1000 landscape. App content only, no browser/device frame, no collage, no numbered option label. Optimistic, charming, premium, industrious. This is a playable mid-game screen, not a marketing page. Attached current-board screenshot is a FUNCTIONAL REFERENCE ONLY: retain recognizable USA railway geography and the gameplay ingredients, thoroughly redesign the style and layout. Current failures to solve: terrain overpowers route legibility, overlays obscure map cities, bottom status obscured by hand, clipped market. Board must be the hero, fully contained and unobstructed, imagined as a performant Three.js scene with subtle physical depth and beautifully lit small train pieces; typography/controls are crisp flat UI overlaid in dedicated areas outside map. Board represents USA and southern Canada with recognizable city positions, labeled city nodes and route segments of different lengths and distinct colors; mostly unclaimed routes plus several claimed routes with miniature trains. All edges fit screen with generous breathing room. Realistic game content: heading "Railbound", "Classic USA", player "You" 24 pts / 32 trains and opponent "Maya" 18 pts / 35 trains, state "Your turn", prompt "Draw cards or claim a route"; five face-up train market cards (blue, ivory, red, wild, black) plus a blind deck; hand with counts red 4, blue 3, yellow 2, wild 1; destination tickets "Portland — Phoenix" 11 and "Chicago — Santa Fe" 9; one "Draw tickets" control; subtle settings/history affordances. No unrelated features, no invented currencies or progress dashboards. Train cards use unmistakable large color areas and fine train illustrations, color symbols as well as hues. At most two typography families, body comfortably readable at 14–16px equivalent. Clear hierarchy, intentional imagery, purposeful spacing; no nested dashboard panels or excessive cards except actual playing cards. Treat all content as simulated game state. Do not put the word Three.js or performance metrics into the product UI.

## 1

Direction name: Daylight Rail. Completely modern Scandinavian editorial tabletop aesthetic, absolutely no Victorian ornaments or science-fiction. Warm chalk canvas, saturated tomato red/cobalt/ochre accents, dark ink typography with contemporary geometric sans. Friendly confident editorial identity, careful asymmetric alignment. Main layout: thin generous header with brand at left and compact player score rows at right; a large almost top-down softly modeled pale mint/ivory USA board across upper 70%; bottom horizontal play tray divided by simple spacing into colorful train hand left and face-up market right. A slim left column beside board for two neat destination ticket slips and clear turn instruction, without boxed dashboard look. The map is a crafted contemporary birch relief puzzle with very restrained topography, clear pill route segments and small satin-painted wooden train miniatures. Tiny stylized mountains only far away from routes, calm pale blue water. Soft daylight contact shadows, tactile physical craftsmanship, airy flat editorial text, refined geometry. Card artwork minimalist modern rail travel posters. Smooth interaction suggested by one blue card gently lifted above its row and a restrained selected-route accent. No dramatic camera tilt. Optimism through light, color, and friendly details.

## 2

Cool pearl-white / ice-blue spatial canvas, deep midnight-blue lettering, fresh teal and electric coral accent, hints of warm sunlight. No Victorian motifs, no wood or parchment. Large central shallow 3D floating USA topographic terrain plate with translucent ice edges and clean raised white landforms, elegant mint/coral rails and tiny sleek bullet-train pieces. Camera near-orthographic with subtle depth, all labels upright and readable. Layout deliberately differs from modern: compact vertical navigation and player score rail at left; enormous central map at upper-middle; right vertical card market in a clean dedicated pale blue strip, five portrait cards fully visible, deck at bottom; bottom broad tray with neatly aligned translucent-edged hand cards, two destination tabs on left, centered confident turn cue. Crisp engineered typographic grid with fine technical rules and a few quiet coordinate-like ticks. No meaningless graphs/gauges or invented stats. Card art sleek transport concept illustrations with large solid color bands. Restrained edge illumination, one luminous route selection; no bloom haze or excessive glow. This should feel like a joyful premium strategy game for a hopeful world of abundant clean rail travel.

## 3

Direction name: The Railway Society. Rich private railway club, upper-class Victorian industrial elegance with mahogany, deep British racing green leather, warm glowing ivory inlays and restrained champagne brass. Optimistic warm evening lamplight, jewel enamel pieces, assured and charming; never dingy, ominous or brown muddy. Different from a paper atlas: the board is a magnificent bespoke cabinetmaker's instrument, a shallow 3D oval-cornered walnut and brass game table surface in central screen, nearly top-down, with an inset mint/ivory geographic USA marquetry map, ocean in deep desaturated teal, very subtle terrain. Finely machined multi-colored enamel route sockets and tiny brass-detailed locomotive/carriage miniatures on claimed routes. Refined serif identity and crisp restrained sans controls. Layout distinctly symmetrical like a club gaming table: centered small Railbound masthead and turn instruction above map; elegant horizontal player score plaques at top corners, no portraits dominating; central map large and fully visible; lower front edge has a green leather rack with colorful elegant illustrated train hand cards fanned very slightly, counts clearly above each card; FIVE market cards arranged in a single horizontal row along right lower section plus blind deck, all distinct from hand with a small label; two ivory destination slips in slim left side ledger strip, and understated Draw tickets control. Brass fine-line dividers rather than piles of panel borders. Beautiful cream label plaques, precision engraved small train motifs, generous spacing. Avoid gears, pipes, bolts everywhere, ornate fantasy frames, garish casino look, excessive shine. One card elevated in a believable soft shadow and one claimed route catching light communicate motion potential.

## 4

Terrain very quiet so colorful route lengths pop. Composition: elegant book-like masthead reading Railbound with tiny engraved locomotive emblem, thin score strip under it; expansive map on left 76%; right margin resembles a well-designed cream conductor's ledger showing turn instruction and five tidy colorful market cards in a vertical stack; lower horizontal player's rack of beautifully printed colored train cards with large counts, two readable engraved destination ticket slips beside it. Plenty of negative space, highly crafted borders only at structural boundaries, subtle corner flourishes and tasteful small botanical or railway engravings on cards. Lifted card and glint on claimed train suggest crisp tasteful animations. Mood: cultured, enterprising, gracious, warm spring morning, grand expedition beginning. Premium stationery meets meticulous physical board game.
