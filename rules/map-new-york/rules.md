# New York

[rulebook PDF](rules.pdf) · [map reference](new-york-map.png) · [source metadata](sources.json)

Original implementation brief; consult the PDF for complete rules and edge cases.

A compact taxi map. Destination tickets reward connected locations, while tourist sites provide an additional scoring opportunity. Use this edition's small card deck, taxi supply, ticket draws, and end condition rather than assuming the full-size USA values.
