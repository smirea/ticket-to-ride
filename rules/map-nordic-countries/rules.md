# Nordic Countries

[rulebook PDF](rules.pdf) · [map reference](nordic-countries-map.png) · [source metadata](sources.json)

Original implementation brief; consult the PDF for complete rules and edge cases.

A standalone northern European map for a small player count. Locomotives have special tunnel and ferry restrictions, and the long Murmansk connection has its own payment rule. Use the edition's ticket handling and completed-ticket bonus rather than inheriting USA defaults.

Map reference: Board shown with components; some detail may be obscured.

Before implementation: Complete destination/card inventory and machine-readable route graph have not been transcribed or verified.
