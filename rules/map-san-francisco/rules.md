Original implementation brief; consult the PDF for complete rules and edge cases.

A compact cable-car map with collectible tourism tokens. Tokens are obtained through qualifying route claims and scored as collections. Verify initial token placement, collection eligibility, duplicate handling, and the set-scoring table directly from the rulebook.
