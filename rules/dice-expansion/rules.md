# Dice Expansion

[rulebook PDF](rules.pdf) · [map reference](dice-expansion-map.png) · [source metadata](sources.json)

Original implementation brief; consult the PDF for complete rules and edge cases.

Replaces the normal train-card economy with dice-based actions. Players roll and optionally reroll to obtain route symbols and tokens. Track token limits, route-length payment, tunnel and ferry exceptions, and the map-specific treatment of stations or other special features.

Read first: [usa-classic](../usa-classic/rules.md).

Map reference: Expansion has no separate geographic board. See base entry provenance.

Before implementation: Complete destination/card inventory and machine-readable route graph have not been transcribed or verified.
