# Paris

[rulebook PDF](rules.pdf) · [map reference](paris-map.png) · [source metadata](sources.json)

Original implementation brief; consult the PDF for complete rules and edge cases.

Claiming eligible blue, white, and red routes can retain matching payment cards toward a French flag. Completing a three-color flag scores four points and clears that collection. Wild cards cannot be retained. Track flag cards separately from the player's hand and normal discard pile.
