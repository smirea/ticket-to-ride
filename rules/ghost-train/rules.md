# Ghost Train

Original implementation brief; consult the PDF for complete rules and edge cases.

A family game using haunted locations and carriage routes. Progress is measured through completed tickets, with a thematic bonus objective and simplified drawing rules. Implement its ticket replacement and victory conditions separately from the standard point-scoring game.
