# Amsterdam

[rulebook PDF](rules.pdf) · [map reference](amsterdam-map.png) · [source metadata](sources.json)

Original implementation brief; consult the PDF for complete rules and edge cases.

A compact cart network with hidden delivery contracts. Claiming a route marked with carts awards one merchandise card regardless of route length. Merchandise holdings contribute an additional competitive bonus. Implement the merchandise tie rules and contract scoring alongside the compact transport-card economy.
