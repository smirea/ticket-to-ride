# USA 1910

[rulebook PDF](rules.pdf) · [map reference](usa-1910-map.png) · [source metadata](sources.json)

Original implementation brief; consult the PDF for complete rules and edge cases.

Adds destination tickets and three USA variants with different deck composition and scoring. Separate 1910, Big Cities, and Mega setup, ticket draw counts, and bonus selection. Card replacements and historical ticket corrections should be represented as an edition-specific deck.
