# Rails & Sails: Great Lakes

[rulebook PDF](rules.pdf) · [map reference](rails-and-sails-great-lakes-map.png) · [source metadata](sources.json)

Original implementation brief; consult the PDF for complete rules and edge cases.

The Great Lakes side combines land and water routes with separate vehicle supplies. Harbor construction and ticket-linked harbor scoring are central. Follow this side's setup and route-payment rules, especially the unusual route between Duluth and Buffalo; do not inherit all World-side settings.
