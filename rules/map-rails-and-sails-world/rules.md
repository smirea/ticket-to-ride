# Rails & Sails: World

[rulebook PDF](rules.pdf) · [map reference](rails-and-sails-world-map.png) · [source metadata](sources.json)

Original implementation brief; consult the PDF for complete rules and edge cases.

Players manage separate train and ship supplies and card decks. Harbors score from completed tickets involving their locations. The world board also introduces ordered tour tickets. Model supply exchanges, double-ship cards, harbor payments, and connectivity across the map boundary.
