# Europa 1912

[rulebook PDF](rules.pdf) · [map reference](europa-1912-map.png) · [source metadata](sources.json)

Original implementation brief; consult the PDF for complete rules and edge cases.

Adds Europe ticket variants plus warehouses and depots. Warehouses accumulate cards, and reaching a depot can allow a player to collect stored cards under the stated conditions. Keep the ticket variants independently selectable from the warehouse module.

Read first: [europe-classic](../europe-classic/rules.md).

Map reference: Expansion has no separate geographic board. See base entry provenance.

Before implementation: Complete destination/card inventory and machine-readable route graph have not been transcribed or verified.
