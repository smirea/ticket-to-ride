# USA Classic

[rulebook PDF](rules.pdf) · [map reference](usa-classic-map.png) · [source metadata](sources.json)

Original implementation brief; consult the PDF for complete rules and edge cases.

Classic North American rules. The three turn choices are taking train cards, claiming one route, or drawing tickets. Separate route scoring, ticket success or failure, and the longest continuous path bonus. The board reference is an early printing with an 80-point score track; it is not a scan of the 2015 edition.

Map reference: First edition photo; score track differs from later editions.

Before implementation: Complete destination/card inventory and machine-readable route graph have not been transcribed or verified.

Catalog reference: [Ticket to Ride](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/9209/ticket-to-ride).
